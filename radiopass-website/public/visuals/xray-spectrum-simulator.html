Live physics notes

    Diagram key — what are we seeing?

    Bremsstrahlung — the continuous curve

    The smooth hump. Incident electrons are decelerated by the pull of the target nucleus, and the energy they lose leaves as an X-ray. Any fraction of an electron's energy can be lost this way, so the result is a continuum running all the way up to the set kVp.

    Characteristic radiation — the spikes

    Sharp lines at fixed energies. An incident electron ejects an inner K-shell electron; an outer electron drops into the vacancy and the difference in binding energies leaves as one photon. The energies belong to the target element, never to the kVp — the kVp only decides whether they appear at all.

    The axes — quantity against quality

    Vertical, quantity: how many photons. The area under the curve is the total output. Horizontal, quality: photon energy. Where the curve meets the axis on the right is the maximum energy, and that point is set by the kVp and nothing else.
