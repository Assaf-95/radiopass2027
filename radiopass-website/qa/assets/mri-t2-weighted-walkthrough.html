    Tissue Signal Comparison — Fat, Water / CSF, Muscle

    Final T2-Weighted Image

    CSF in the ventricles and sulci appears bright, muscle appears relatively dark, and fat is intermediate. Every brightness value is computed from the signal equation for that tissue — none is assigned by hand. Brain parenchyma is rendered from the soft-tissue entry, because white and grey matter are deliberately not modelled on this page; they appear on the Sequence Summary.
