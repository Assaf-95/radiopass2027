Hydrogen Only: Spin, Precession, Phase, and RF Pulse

Single diagram with flat axes only: Z is horizontal and X is vertical. No oblique perspective and no full-page crossing lines.

Use the buttons in order: one proton, few out-of-phase, apply B0 (Larmor precession in phase), then apply RF pulse.

Mode: single proton

Teaching Line

One hydrogen nucleus behaves like a tiny magnet. Its magnetic moment precesses in a circle.

Step Flow

1. Single hydrogen proton with spin and dipole
2. A few protons with different phases
3. Apply B0 and show Larmor precession in phase (teaching simplification)
4. Apply RF pulse and observe transverse signal in coil
