Ultrasound physics

Acoustic Impedance and Partial Reflection

Follow an ultrasound pulse as it reaches a tissue boundary. Drag the mismatch control to see how much energy returns to the probe versus continuing deeper.

Acoustic Impedance

Acoustic impedance is the resistance a medium provides to sound propagation.

  • It depends on tissue density and sound velocity
  • It is denoted by Z
  • Adjacent tissues with different Z values create echoes

Reflection at an Interface

When a pulse meets a boundary, part of its energy reflects and part transmits.

  • Larger impedance mismatch means a brighter echo
  • Smaller mismatch means most energy keeps travelling
  • Soft-tissue boundaries usually produce partial reflection

Through-Transmission

  • The unreflected portion continues deeper
  • Transmission allows imaging beyond the first boundary
Image brightness rises with reflection strength. Reflection strength rises with impedance difference.
