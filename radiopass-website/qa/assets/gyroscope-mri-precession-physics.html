Current focus: B0 alignment

Section 1 - Gyroscope Analogy

Just as gravity causes a spinning gyroscope to precess, an external magnetic field (B0) causes hydrogen nuclei to precess.

Section 2 - Hydrogen Nucleus as a Tiny Magnet

Because hydrogen nuclei have charge and spin, they generate a magnetic field and behave like tiny magnets.

Section 3 - Application of B0 (Main Field) and Precession

F = K × B0 F = Larmor frequency K = gyro (gyromagnetic ratio)
Parallel spins: low energy Anti-parallel spins: high energy Precession around Z-axis

Section 4 - RF Excitation, Relaxation, and Coil Signal

Net longitudinal magnetisation (Mz) is tipped into transverse magnetisation (Mxy) by a 90° RF pulse. Mz then regrows by T1 recovery, Mxy decays by T2 decay, and the rotating transverse component induces detectable signal in RF coils.
