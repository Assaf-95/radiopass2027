Key points
  • Dotted boundary rays start from both focal-spot edges and show penumbra boundaries.
  • Shaded band on detector = blurred region (geometric unsharpness, Ug).
  • Dark central band on detector = sharp image core.
  • Penumbra is caused by finite focal spot size: multiple point sources within the focal spot create overlapping edge shadows at object margins.
  • Increasing OID increases magnification and geometric unsharpness.
  • Increasing SID while SOD is fixed increases OID, so magnification and unsharpness rise.
  • Decreasing SOD increases magnification; increasing focal spot size increases penumbra.
