    Final STIR Image

    Scalp fat is now dark while water-containing tissue stays bright. The suppression comes from fat reaching its null point at the chosen inversion time, calculated from its T1 — not from darkening it afterwards. Because this depends on short T1 rather than chemical identity, STIR is unsuitable for assessing gadolinium enhancement. Brain parenchyma is rendered from the soft-tissue entry, because white and grey matter are deliberately not modelled on this page; they appear on the Sequence Summary.
