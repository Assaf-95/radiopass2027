    Final FLAIR Image

    Free CSF in the ventricles and sulci is now dark, while the T2-weighted readout keeps other high-water tissue relatively bright. The dark CSF is produced by the calculated longitudinal magnetisation at the chosen inversion time — not by darkening it afterwards. Brain parenchyma is rendered from the soft-tissue entry, because white and grey matter are deliberately not modelled on this page; they appear on the Sequence Summary.
