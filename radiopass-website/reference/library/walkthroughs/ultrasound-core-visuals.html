Doppler angle

The measured Doppler shift is greatest when the beam is parallel to flow and approaches zero at 90 degrees.

30 degrees

Acoustic impedance and reflection

A larger impedance mismatch produces a stronger reflected echo. Soft tissue-air interfaces therefore reflect almost all incident ultrasound.

FRCR rule: greater difference in acoustic impedance means greater reflection and less transmission.

Mechanical index and thermal index

MI relates to the likelihood of mechanical effects such as cavitation. TI estimates the potential for tissue heating.

Use ALARA: keep output and dwell time as low as reasonably achievable while obtaining diagnostic information.
