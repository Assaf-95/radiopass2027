High-yield reminders
T1w: short TR + short TE. Fat tends to be bright, water darker.
T2w: long TR + long TE. Water/CSF bright, fat relatively darker.
Scan time: TR x phase steps x NEX (phase matrix drives time).
Chemical shift: along frequency-encoding axis. Shift (pixels) = Δf / BW per pixel.
Contrast control:
TR and TE control weighting. Inversion recovery adds TI for nulling.
FLAIR: nulls CSF. STIR: nulls fat (avoid post-gadolinium).
Artifact triad:
Chemical shift (fat-water frequency difference), susceptibility (field distortion), aliasing (FOV too small).
Spatial resolution:
matrix, FOV, slice thickness define voxel dimensions and partial-volume behavior.
Safety:
MR-safe / conditional / unsafe, SAR limits, projectile risk, RF burns, quench preparedness.
