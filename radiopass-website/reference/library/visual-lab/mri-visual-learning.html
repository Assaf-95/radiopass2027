    Final summary

    1. B0 creates net longitudinal magnetisation.
    2. Spins precess at the Larmor frequency.
    3. RF pulse tips magnetisation into the transverse plane.
    4. Rotating Mxy creates the MR signal.
    5. T1 is recovery of Mz.
    6. T2 is decay of Mxy.
    7. Gradients encode position.
    8. K-space is filled using gradient-controlled signal acquisition.
    9. The final MRI image is reconstructed from k-space.
