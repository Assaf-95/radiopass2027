# Why the bank feels repetitive

The audit found no duplicate questions. What it found is repeated *wording*:

| Count | Question stem |
|---:|---|
| 201 | Identify the structures labelled A to E. |
| 92 | Identify the structures labelled A–E. |
| 32 | Name the structures labelled A to E. |
| 26 | Identify the structure indicated by the arrow. |
| 16 | Identify the structure indicated. |
| 11 | Identify the structure labelled by the arrow. |
| 4 | Name the normal variant |
| 4 | Identify the structure labelled. |

Every one of those uses a different image and asks about different anatomy. Making them
feel distinct is a wording edit, not a deletion. No question is redundant.
